About Me

Teaching English through practical, engaging and learner-centred lessons.

I am an English teacher and TEFL professional with experience teaching learners in both classroom and online environments.

My teaching focuses on practical communication, clear learning objectives and creating opportunities for learners to use English confidently in realistic situations.

I use communicative, learner-centred activities to help students develop speaking skills, build confidence and use English for meaningful communication.

Teaching Philosophy

Creating meaningful opportunities for learners to communicate with confidence.

I believe English is best learned through meaningful communication rather than memorisation alone. Learners should have opportunities to use English in realistic situations where they can express ideas, ask questions and solve problems.

My lessons are learner-centred and communicative, with a clear progression from guided practice to more independent speaking. I use practical situations, questioning, interaction and targeted language support to help learners become more confident and effective communicators.

I also aim to create a supportive learning environment where mistakes are treated as part of the learning process and learners are encouraged to participate actively.
