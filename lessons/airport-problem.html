Handling a Problem at the Airport

A practical English lesson focused on helping learners communicate politely and effectively when dealing with common airport problems.

Lesson Overview

This lesson gives learners the language and confidence needed to ask for help, request information and respond appropriately in common airport situations.

Learning Objectives

  • Ask airport staff for help politely.
  • Ask clear questions to obtain information.
  • Explain a common airport problem.
  • Respond appropriately during a role-play.

Target Language

  • Excuse me, I need some help.
  • I can't find my boarding pass.
  • Could you help me, please?
  • What should I do?
  • Could you repeat that, please?
  • Thank you so much.

Role-Play Activity

Learners practise a realistic airport situation in which a passenger cannot find their boarding pass and must ask airport staff for assistance.

Teaching Materials

The lesson includes a role-play card, language bank, vocabulary resources and classroom activities.
