Handling a Problem at the Airport

A practical English lesson focused on helping learners communicate politely and effectively when dealing with common airport problems.

Lesson Overview

This is a 45-minute B1 Functional English speaking lesson designed for adult English learners. The lesson focuses on helping learners communicate politely and effectively when dealing with common airport problems.

Lesson Details

  • Level: B1 – Intermediate
  • Learners: Adult English Learners
  • Class Size: 1–6 learners
  • Length: 45 minutes
  • Main Skill: Speaking
  • Supporting Skills: Listening and Functional Vocabulary
  • Teaching Approach: Communicative Language Teaching (CLT) using a PPP framework

Lesson Aim

To develop learners' ability to communicate politely and effectively when helping someone who has an airport-related problem.

Learning Objectives

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

  • Recognise and understand useful expressions used when assisting passengers.
  • Use polite functional English to ask questions, clarify information, reassure passengers and offer assistance.
  • Participate in a realistic airport role-play using appropriate language.

Target Language

  • Offering Help: How can I help you?
  • Clarifying: Could you explain what happened?
  • Checking Information: Do you mean that your flight has been cancelled?
  • Reassuring: Don't worry. / I'll see what I can do.
  • Offering Assistance: Let me check that for you.
  • Giving Instructions: Could you please wait here for a moment?
  • Optional Extension: Is there anything else I can help you with?

Lesson Stages

  • 1. Warm-up / Context — 5 min: Introduce airport situations and activate learners' background knowledge.
  • 2. Presentation / Elicitation — 8 min: Elicit and introduce useful expressions through realistic situations.
  • 3. Meaning, Form & Pronunciation — 7 min: Use CCQs and pronunciation practice to clarify the target language.
  • 4. Controlled Practice — 7 min: Practise the language through a controlled worksheet activity.
  • 5. Guided Speaking Practice — 5 min: Learners use airport problem cards and practise the target expressions.
  • 6. Freer Role-play — 8 min: Learners communicate naturally without a script.
  • 7. Feedback & Error Correction — 3 min: Review successful language and correct important errors.
  • 8. Plenary / Exit Check — 2 min: Learners respond independently to short airport situations.

Total lesson time: 45 minutes
